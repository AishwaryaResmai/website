About Vishwas Enterprises

A Trusted Recovery Partner for Leading Banks & NBFCs

Established in 2019 in Hyderabad, Vishwas Enterprises provides high-integrity, performance-driven delinquent debt recovery solutions across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Institutional Debt Resolution Powered by Experience

Vishwas Enterprises was founded in 2019 to bridge the gap between rigorous banking recovery targets and dignified, compliant customer communication. Headquartered in Suchitra, Hyderabad with our strategic branch at Navakethan Complex, Secunderabad, we operate across all districts of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

We partner with major Indian commercial banks, regional lenders, and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), providing full-lifecycle delinquency support from early bucket telecalling to skip tracing and specialized outstation ground visits.

Established in 2019: Continuous operational track record in banking and financial-sector recovery.
Two Operational Centers: Suchitra registered office (20 seats) and Navakethan Complex branch (80 seats).
Responsible Business Practices: Zero harassment tolerance, structured payment settlement discussions, and full adherence to client codes of conduct.

Specialized Portfolio Solutions

Comprehensive Collection & Recovery Services

Tailored portfolio management solutions covering retail, secured, and commercial debt across early delinquency, hard buckets, and written-off books.

Personal Loan Collections

Disciplined telecalling and structured field counseling for unsecured retail loans across early to late-stage delinquency buckets.

Credit Card Collections

High-velocity recovery for revolving unsecured credit card accounts, curbing roll-rates and facilitating authorized bank settlement discussions.

Business Loan Collections

Specialized recovery for SME and MSME working capital loans with promoter consultations, financial restructuring, and cash-flow evaluations.

Consumer Loan Collections

Retail consumer durables, appliance finance, and lifestyle loans resolution with intensive localized follow-up and prompt receipting.

X-Sell Collections

Dedicated delinquency handling for pre-approved, top-up, and cross-sell banking products requiring targeted borrower engagement.

Two-Wheeler Loan Collections

Two-wheeler retail asset recovery combining geo-tagged field visits, borrower location tracking, and amicable overdue clearances.

Skip Tracing

Lawful location intelligence and address restoration for non-contactable, relocated, or untraceable borrowers without privacy breach.

Outstation Field Visits

Deep field connectivity across Tier-2, Tier-3 cities, and rural taluks throughout Telangana and Andhra Pradesh with verified in-person reports.

Portfolio Monitoring & Supervision

End-to-end delinquency bucket surveillance, weekly performance reviews, automated daily MIS, and payment settlement discussions.
