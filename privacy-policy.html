Privacy Policy & Regulatory Compliance

Our commitment to institutional data protection, borrower dignity, and strict adherence to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Practice Code.

1. Institutional Mandate & Scope

Vishwas Enterprises ("the Agency") is a licensed and empanelled banking collection and recovery agency established in 2019, headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana. We operate strictly on behalf of formal institutional lenders, including scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks, and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

This policy outlines our procedures regarding institutional business information, online communications, and the safeguarding of borrower confidentiality in full alignment with the Master Directions of the Reserve Bank of India.

2. Absolute Protection of Individual Borrower Information

In compliance with Indian banking statutes and customer privacy rights:

  • Zero Public Exposure: Vishwas Enterprises never hosts, publishes, indexes, or reveals confidential borrower records, individual loan account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers, or contact details on this website or any public domain.
  • Encrypted Operational Data: All loan data provided by client banks for delinquent recovery is stored within air-gapped, access-controlled workstations at our Suchitra and Secunderabad operational facilities.
  • Strict Prohibition on Inquiry Form: Our online business enquiry form is engineered solely for institutional representatives. We strictly instruct users never to transmit individual debtor financial records via online forms.

3. Recovery Agent Code of Conduct & RBI Guidelines

Our workforce of 53 personnel (including 2 supervisors, 2 back-office staff, 45 telecallers, and 4 field executives) is trained and audited against the following regulatory principles:

  • Permitted Communication Hours: Borrowers are contacted strictly between 07:00 AM and 07:00 PM. No communication occurs outside permissible statutory windows.
  • Zero Harassment: We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against intimidation, abusive language, or harassment. All customer interactions emphasize consultative, dignified negotiation.
  • Settlement Discussions: All payment settlement discussions (including One-Time Settlement / OTS terms) are conducted strictly in accordance with the specific written mandates and code of conduct approved by the respective client bank.
  • DRA Certification: Ground recovery executives are background-verified through police verification and KYC records prior to field deployment.

4. Institutional B2B Information Collected

Information gathered via our website enquiry forms is limited to corporate contact details (Bank Name, Representative Name, Work Email, Contact Mobile, Service Category, and Project Brief). This data is utilized solely for vendor empanelment evaluation and proposal communication. We do not sell, rent, or trade corporate contact data to third parties.
