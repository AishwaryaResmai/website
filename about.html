About Vishwas Enterprises

Established in 2019 in Hyderabad, Telangana, delivering professional banking collections, portfolio supervision, and compliant recoveries.

Company Background

Founded in 2019 in Hyderabad

Vishwas Enterprises is a specialized banking collections and recovery agency headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana. Since our inception in 2019, our operational footprint has expanded across both Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, earning trusted empanelment with leading commercial banks, private lenders, and NBFCs.

We provide structured, ethical, and high-velocity delinquent portfolio resolution. By upholding the highest standards of the Reserve Bank of India’s Fair Practice Code and relevant bank codes of conduct, we protect the institutional brand reputation of our client partners while delivering consistent liquidation yields.

Core Operating Regions: Full coverage across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
Dual Operational Hubs: Registered Office at Suchitra (20 seats) & Branch at Navakethan Complex, Secunderabad (80 seats).
Honors & Accolades: 11 Platinum Awards, 6 Gold Awards, and the Vendor Premier League Recovery Certificate.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and ethically compliant debt recovery and portfolio supervision agency in Southern India, setting industry benchmarks in borrower communication, audit compliance, and resolution speed.

Our Mission

Delivering disciplined collection operations through experienced leadership, dedicated manpower, advanced tracking technology, and strict adherence to client and regulatory codes of conduct.
